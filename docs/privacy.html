Privacy Policy

Effective 23 August 2026

FourExHex does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking of any kind.

What the game collects

Nothing. FourExHex has no sign-in, no user profiles, and no telemetry. The app requests no device permissions, and the Android build ships without the INTERNET permission — it cannot make network connections at all.

What is stored on your device

The game saves the following locally, in its own private storage. It never leaves your device unless you choose to send a bug report (below).

  • Game saves and autosaves
  • Settings and preferences
  • Achievement progress
  • Starting maps you create or import
  • The author name you choose when exporting a map, remembered so you don't retype it
  • Diagnostic logs written by the game engine

Uninstalling FourExHex deletes all of it. Saved games can also be deleted individually from within the game.

Bug reports

If you choose to send a bug report from inside the game, FourExHex prepares a zip file and hands it to your own email or sharing app. Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself — the game has no ability to transmit it on its own. The zip contains:

  • A short diagnostic header: the app version and your device model and OS version
  • Your most recent autosave
  • The game's recent diagnostic log

Reports go to foobarzalot@gmail.com and are used only to diagnose and fix the reported problem. They are not shared with anyone else, and are not used for advertising or profiling. You can review and edit the message, or remove the attachment, before sending.

Sharing maps

The map editor can export a map you have built. As with bug reports, this happens only when you ask for it, and the game hands the file to your device's own sharing or save dialog — you choose where it goes, and it is not sent to the developer.

An exported map contains the map itself and, if you enter one, an author name. The author name is optional and is whatever you type; it travels inside any map file you share. Maps other people send you can be imported the same way, and are stored only on your device.

Children

FourExHex collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The game bundles no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or social SDKs. No data is shared with third parties because none is collected.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
